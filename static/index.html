Preferred Vendor Management System

Three connected screens covering the full vendor lifecycle, from a first application through to an approved vendor working inside NetSuite. Click into each one and use it as you would on the day.

01 · INTAKE

New Vendor Application

What a prospective vendor sees. Guided, validated, and structured so the information arrives complete the first time.

  • Step by step company, contact and compliance capture
  • Insurance, W-9 and certification uploads
  • Validation before submission, not after

02 · VENDOR

Vendor Portal

Where an approved vendor lives afterwards. Status, documents and renewals in one place, so the Port stops chasing paperwork by email.

  • Application status and approval history
  • Document library with expiry tracking
  • Self service updates and renewal reminders

03 · PORT STAFF

PVMS inside NetSuite

The internal view. Review queue, scoring, approvals and reporting, native to NetSuite rather than bolted alongside it.

  • Review queue with status and ageing
  • Vendor record, scoring and approval workflow
  • Dashboards and compliance reporting
